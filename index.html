Good rewards start with clear rules.

  1. Choose your cards. Real-card entries link to issuer terms and disclose excluded rewards. Fictional examples are marked Demo. No account or sensitive information required.
  2. Describe the purchase. Select the merchant’s actual rewards category, amount, and payment channel. Merchant names alone do not guarantee eligibility.
  3. Compare net value. We apply modeled rates, remaining caps, activation, your point valuation, and foreign fees. Ties are shown as equal-value choices.

Cap spending is manually tracked, not synced, and does not auto-reset. These reference estimates are not financial advice or a complete issuer-benefit comparison. Unverified entries require checking current issuer terms. Housing rewards, relationship boosts and some portal bonuses are excluded.
